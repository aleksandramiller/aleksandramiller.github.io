    • October 2026

      Redesigning the content production workflow for Mosaic Creative Group to increase efficiency, reduce unreviewed content and unapproved AI use.

        Background

      • Mosaic Creative Group is a creative agency that runs client work from brief to publication. Copywriters had begun using AI tools, but prompts and approvals were not being recorded.

        • Business Problem

      • Content often reached clients and the public without a brand-voice check, and legal review happened only when someone raised a concern. AI use, permissions and prompts were untracked, even though some clients require disclosure or prohibit AI altogether.

        • Steps

      • Mapping the current six-step process to identify actors, time, and error points, separating case facts from assumptions.
      • Designing a nine-step runbook specifying the actor, tool, input, prompt template, expected output, review test, and failure action for every step. AI is used only where the client permits it, and its use is disclosed.
      • Building in two missing checkpoints: a brand-voice review by the creative director before anything is shown to a client, and a legal review before anything is published. Pieces that fail either one return for revision.
      • Creating a content calendar log that tracks client ID, asset, channel, owner, review status, AI disclosure, AI prompts used, and publication date.
      • Comparing the current and redesigned processes on time, cost and risk using stated assumptions, and checking the design decision to require sources for claims and a legal review against the FTC's advertising guidance on claim substantiation.

        • Recommendations

      • Require a brand-voice review for every asset before client presentation, not only for larger accounts.
      • Require documented legal clearance before any asset is published.
      • Record AI use, disclosure and prompts for every asset, and treat AI as prohibited until written permission is confirmed.
      • Assign clear ownership at each step and audit the content calendar periodically to prevent checkpoints from becoming formalities.

        • Results

      • Delivered a complete workflow design document covering the runbook, workflow diagram, verification plan, and a time, cost and risk comparison. Based on my assumptions, the redesign adds roughly 45 minutes and $68.69 per asset in exchange for lower risk of unapproved content, legal issues and undisclosed AI use.
